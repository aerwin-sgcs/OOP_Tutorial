Ironwall

An OOP auto-battler. Three defenders — Warrior, Archer and Thief — hold the line against three waves of rogues marching on the castle. The same inheritance and polymorphism you've been learning, brought to life with images and animation.

The OOP inside

  • Inheritance — every character descends from one shared GameCharacter class.
  • Multi-level inheritance — GameCharacter → Defender / Enemy → the six characters.
  • Polymorphism — everyone has attack(), but each does something different.
